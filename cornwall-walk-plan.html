Training plan

Practice walk log

    Tips and local hills

    Rules of thumb

    Walk at a pace where you can chat. On long days the slower walker sets the pace.

    Strength twice a week, about 20 minutes: squats, step-ups, lunges, calf raises, glute bridges and a plank.

    Wear your trip boots on the long walks, try poles on descents, and do one long walk in the rain.

    Practise eating and drinking on the move. Keep the Sennen rest day gentle. Before ramping up, a quick check with your GP is sensible.

    Hills near home

    Steepest ground: High Elms, Downe and Cudham to the south-west (the Cudham Circular, about 16 km). Chalk paths are slippery when wet.

    South-east: Court Road towards Chelsfield, Pratts Bottom and the Downs. A Ramblers day walk from Orpington station reports just over 500 m of climbing.

    Wet days: repeat a paved climb 4–6 times, or use stairs or a treadmill on a steep incline. Rushmore Hill is a long, steady road climb but can be busy. Avoid Old Hill: narrow, no pavement.

    In winter use hi-vis and a head torch on lanes, and save the narrow lanes for daylight weekends.
